Chicken sizing

How big is a bird?

Our birds dress out between three and six pounds. Pick what fits how you cook. Smaller for a weeknight roast, bigger for a crowd or for stock after.

  • Small 3 – 4 lbs dressed
  • Medium 4 – 5 lbs dressed
  • Large 5 – 6 lbs dressed
Weights are approximate. These are whole animals, not a molded product. If you need an exact weight for a date, email us and we'll tell you what we have.
